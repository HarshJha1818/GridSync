About GridSync

GridSync is an AI-powered cross-utility coordination platform designed for Florida's power (FPL, Duke, Co-ops) and water utilities (WASD, BCWWS). It tracks capital projects across a strict logistics radar to eliminate Right-of-Way road gridlock and enforce "Dig Once" municipal excavation efficiency.
